NotesOn mobile the Join row stays pinned at the top of the club page while you scroll; on desktop it sits right-aligned in the header. About lives under the member count with a View more button (no About tab). Tabs are Posts (default) and Events. Any student can start a club (public, listed in Browse) or a group (smaller, can stay invite-only) from the directory — not just officers; new ones show “Pending review” until approved. Fair mode (swipe cards) is for Involvement Fair. Announcements are rate-limited and go to a digest by default.
