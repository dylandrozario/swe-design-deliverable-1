Key decisions1) Desktop nav lives in a left sidebar (Home, Events, Market, Clubs, Notifications, Profile) and messages float bottom-right like Instagram. 2) One sticky primary action per detail screen. 3) Templates and presets replace typing. 4) Digests and rate limits keep clubs from spamming. 5) Verified badge plus campus meetup spots build trust. 6) The calendar unifies RSVPs and meetings.
Risks and first testsClub spam (1 announcement per week, digest default). Scams (verified-only DMs, report and block, safe spots). Cold start (seed Welcome Week and Game Day, recruit 10 officer clubs). Test with 5 freshmen, 5 officers and 5 sellers: success, time and taps. Iterate filter labels and safe-spot presets first.
