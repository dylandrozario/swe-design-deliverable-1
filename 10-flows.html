Key decisions0) Messaging is connections-only, everywhere (Market sellers included: “Connect to message”). Notifications cover new connection requests, RSVPs to events you posted and new messages, in-app and by email. 1) Desktop nav lives in a left sidebar (Home, Calendar, Events, Market, Clubs, Profile) plus a Create button (Post/Event/Listing/Club or group); messages float bottom-right like Instagram; notifications are a bell dropdown and settings a gear next to the profile picture, not sidebar items. 2) One sticky primary action per detail screen — Events and Market now have a full web detail page on desktop too. 3) Templates and presets replace typing. 4) Digests and rate limits keep clubs from spamming. 5) Verified badge plus campus meetup spots build trust. 6) The calendar unifies RSVPs and meetings and now has its own page. 7) Any student can start a club or private group, not just officers, pending a review queue. 8) There’s no standalone notifications feed page — every notification links to what it’s about.
Risks and first testsClub spam (1 announcement per week, digest default). Scams (verified-only DMs, report and block, safe spots). Cold start (seed Welcome Week and Game Day, recruit 10 officer clubs). Unmoderated club/group creation (review queue before public listing; exact approval path for non-officers is still open). Test with 5 freshmen, 5 officers and 5 sellers: success, time and taps. Iterate filter labels and safe-spot presets first.
