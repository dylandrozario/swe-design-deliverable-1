Screen 01: Onboarding & Student Verification

1-Minute BC Verification & Cold-Start Feed Personalization
Google OAuth @bc.edu Gate Major Selection Instant Trust
Key Capabilities
  • @bc.edu Google OAuth: Single-sign-on verification with no password friction. Non-BC accounts are instantly rejected with an inline error state.
  • Academic Profile: Class year picker ('26–'29, Grad), Primary Major selection, and optional Second Major/Minor.
  • Cold-Start Solution: Interest chips populate recommended clubs and classmates so Home is never empty on first login.
Design Decisions & UX
  • One Decision Per Step: Progressive disclosure reduces cognitive load to under 60 seconds total.
  • Chips Over Typing: Quick pill selectors for year and interests eliminate tedious mobile typing.
  • Skip Safeguards: Non-essential steps provide skip options to get returning or hurried students into the app quickly.
Mobile vs. Desktop
  • Mobile (390px): 5-step full-screen wizard with top step indicator pills and sticky bottom action buttons.
  • Desktop (1280px): Split-screen layout with full-bleed BC maroon brand hero on left and clean step card on right.
💡
Presenter Takeaway: Onboarding establishes absolute campus trust from day one by restricting access strictly to authenticated @bc.edu accounts, while solving the cold-start problem through interest tagging.
